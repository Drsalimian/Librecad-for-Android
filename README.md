Getting Started
Installing Librecad may take approximately 20 minutes. To ensure a smooth installation, you can switch to other apps while the process continues in the background.

How to start:

1. Grant notification permission by tapping 'Enable Notification'.
2. Start your session by tapping 'Enter Application'. 
   A notification will appear, confirming that the app is successfully working in the background.

File Management

Managing Your Files
To import and manage your projects, use the 'Librecad Files Folder' button.

Importing Files:
On the folder page, tap 'Add File' and select your files from the Android picker. Note: Simply selecting a file is not enough; you must import it into this folder.

Storage & Exporting:
Your files are safely kept in the internal Librecad_Files folder. From this page, you can:

· Open folders
· Delete files
· Save them to your Android storage using 'Export'

Good to know: Files in this folder are not deleted when you update the app or files.


Navigation & Toolbar

ZOOM Key Controls
Master your workspace using the ZOOM key on the Librecad toolbar:

· When ZOOM: OFF
  · Touch: Selects Librecad tools.
  · Drag: Moves the cursor or performs normal dragging.
· When ZOOM: ON
  · Pinch (2 fingers): Zoom in or out.
  · Drag (1 finger): Pan the image.
  · Touch: Still selects tools.
  · Hold Touch: Performs a right-click action.
